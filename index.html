One Hundred & Fifty Turns Upon the Road
History

Named for the grandmother of Ebanna, Serra Troupe has traveled Pern for one hundred and fifty turns. Founded in Turn 2686 by master harpers Alphonse and Serra, who loved travel and performance above all things, they raised their family on the roads and gathered talented souls to their caravan along the way. Leadership has passed through the family ever since, each generation raised with the expectation of one day taking over.

Hogarth and Ebanna took the reins in Turn 2796. By Turn 2830, their success allowed them to expand their route to include the Southern Continent. Then, in the summer of Turn 2836, sickness swept the caravan — taking many of the elder members, including Hoban's parents. Hoban now leads the troupe.

Historically, they have hired members of the Ashwulf Caravan to guard and guide them through the South, though that contract may require renegotiation in the wake of recent losses.

Jaemi Harper · Actor · Seasonal Performer
32 · He/Him · FC: Willy Monfret
Little is truly known about the enigmatic Harper known as Jaemi — a man as much myth as he is flesh. His past is a patchwork of contradictions; each tale he spins counters the last, some grandiose, others almost plausible, yet none quite touching the truth. He wields ambiguity like a blade, leaving those who listen enthralled and reluctant to pry too deeply, lest they unravel the magic he so effortlessly weaves.

18 · He/Him · FC: Bretman Rock

Either way, his little conspiracy has become the centrepiece of his act and the envy of many a handler twice his age.
